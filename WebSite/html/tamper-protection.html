Tamper Protection

Tamper Protection stops programs — including malware — from changing or disabling your Defender settings without your knowledge. It’s the lock on the lock. This one is essential. Windows does not allow any program to turn it on for you, so it is worth doing by hand today — the steps are below.

Why this matters

Malware’s first move is often to disable your antivirus. Tamper Protection prevents that. When it’s on, nothing can change your Defender settings except you, through the Windows Security app — not scripts, not other programs, not malware.

If Tamper Protection is off and you didn’t turn it off yourself, that’s worth paying attention to. It could mean something on your PC already tried to disable your defenses.

How to check it yourself

  1. Press the Windows key and type Windows Security, then press Enter.
  2. Click Virus & threat protection.
  3. Under Virus & threat protection settings, click Manage settings.
  4. Scroll down to Tamper Protection. Confirm it is set to On.

How to change it yourself

  1. Open Windows Security and go to Virus & threat protection > Manage settings.
  2. Scroll to Tamper Protection and click the toggle to turn it On.
  3. Windows may ask for administrator permission. Click Yes when prompted.

What to expect

No restart required. Tamper Protection takes effect immediately.

Tamper Protection was off and I didn’t turn it off. Run a full Defender scan: open Windows Security, click Virus & threat protection, then Quick scan or Scan options > Full scan. If Malwarebytes is installed, run a scan there too.
