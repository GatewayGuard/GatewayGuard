Windows Widgets

Windows Widgets is the news, weather, and sports panel that appears on your taskbar. It sends information about what you read and click back to Microsoft. Turning it off removes the panel and stops that data sharing.

▶ Your decision

What Checkup found and did

Found:Checkup reviews Windows Widgets on your taskbar. It can turn it off with your permission.
Action taken:Checkup turned off Windows Widgets with your approval. If you use and enjoy the Widgets panel, you can turn it back on at any time — the steps are below.

Why you might want this off

Widgets sends your reading and browsing habits back to Microsoft. The panel is powered by Microsoft Start, and it tracks which stories you read, how long you spend on them, and what you click on — to serve you more personalized content. If you do not want Microsoft collecting that information, turning Widgets off is the clean solution.

There is also a background process involved. Widgets uses the Edge browser engine (WebView2) to run even when you are not looking at it. Turning it off frees up that memory and removes a process that was running without you asking for it.

How to check it yourself

  1. Press the Windows key, type Taskbar settings, and press Enter.
  2. Look for Widgets in the list. If the toggle is Off, Widgets is disabled.

How to turn it back on if you want it

  1. Press the Windows key, type Taskbar settings, and press Enter.
  2. Toggle Widgets to On.
  3. The Widgets button reappears on your taskbar immediately. No restart needed.

What to expect

With Widgets off, the weather and news icon disappears from your taskbar. Your PC will use slightly less memory in the background. Everything else on your PC works exactly the same. If you want news or weather, your browser works just as well for that.

This is more than a taste. The panel builds a record of what you read and click, and it keeps a background process running when you are not looking at it. Anything still running is still working. Checkup turns it off with your permission, and you can turn it back on whenever you like.
