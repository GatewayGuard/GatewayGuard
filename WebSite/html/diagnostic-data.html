Diagnostic Data

Windows sends information about how your PC is running back to Microsoft. There are two levels: the minimum needed to keep Windows working, and a broader set that includes information about what you do on your PC. Checkup recommends the minimum.

▶ Your decision

What Checkup found and did

Found:Checkup reviews how much diagnostic data your PC sends to Microsoft. It can limit this to the required minimum with your permission.
Action taken:Checkup set Diagnostic data to Required diagnostic data with your approval. This is the minimum level Windows needs to function and receive updates. It does not affect performance or updates in any way.

Why you might want the minimum

The optional level sends information about what you do on your PC. This includes which apps you use, how long you use them, which features you click on, and details about your browsing. Microsoft uses this to improve Windows — but it is your PC and your data, and you may not want to share it.

The required level sends only what Windows needs to work: basic device information, error reports, and update-related data. Your PC gets the same updates either way. The difference is how much information Microsoft receives about your daily use.

How to set it to the minimum

  1. Press the Windows key, type Diagnostics & feedback, and press Enter.
  2. Under Diagnostic data, you will see two options: Required diagnostic data and Optional diagnostic data.
  3. Select Required diagnostic data. It should already be selected if Checkup ran successfully. If it shows Optional, click Required to change it.

What to expect

No restart required. Windows continues to receive all updates and works exactly the same. The change is that Microsoft receives less information about how you use your PC.

This is more than a taste. Nobody is reading your day — but a record of what you did on your own computer is kept where you cannot see it, for as long as someone decides to keep it, and you were never asked. Turning it down does not erase what is already there; it stops the list getting longer. Checkup makes this change with your permission.
