The Security Guide

The GatewayGuard Windows Security Walkthrough Guide is a PDF you download and work through at your own pace. It covers the settings that matter on a Windows 11 PC — what each one does, and how to check and change it yourself.

It comes in five sizes, from compact to extra-large print. Pick the one that is easiest on your eyes. The words are identical.

GatewayGuard Checkup

Checkup is a program that walks through your Windows 11 security settings one at a time. It explains each one in plain English and asks your permission before it changes anything. Nothing happens without you saying yes.

Run it as many times as you want. It is worth running again whenever something changes on your PC — and every run writes a plain-English record of what it found and what you approved, which you can open at any time.

The full source is included and readable, so you can check exactly what it does. It asks for no account, collects nothing, and sends nothing anywhere.

$19.99 once, for one PC

Annual updates

Once a year Microsoft makes a major update to Windows 11. Settings move, new ones appear, and old ones are retired. That is the best moment to run Checkup again — and it is the moment your copy knows the least about, because it was built before the update existed.

So each year, after Microsoft’s update lands, we publish a refreshed Checkup that knows where everything is in the new version of Windows. It walks your settings again, offers to correct anything the update changed with your approval, and scans your drives again while it is there.

$12.99 a year, for one PC

Taking an update is your choice each year. If you skip one, nothing stops working — the copy you have keeps running exactly as it did.
