Defender Periodic Scanning

If you use Malwarebytes as your main antivirus, Defender steps back and lets it run. Periodic Scanning keeps Defender running quiet monthly checks in the background as a second opinion — so you get two scanners for the price of none.

✓ Checkup can do this for you

What Checkup found and did

Found:Checkup reviews Defender Periodic Scanning. This setting only appears when another antivirus program is active — if Defender is your primary antivirus, the option is hidden because Defender is already scanning continuously. Windows does not allow this one to be changed by a program, so Checkup shows you the exact steps instead.
Action taken:If periodic scanning was off and Malwarebytes was your primary antivirus, Checkup flagged it and offered to turn it on. No changes are made without your approval.

Why this matters

Two scanners catch more than one. Malwarebytes and Defender use different detection methods. Running both means a threat that slips past one may still be caught by the other. With Periodic Scanning on, Defender runs a quiet background check once a month without interfering with Malwarebytes at all.

If Defender is already your primary antivirus — with no Malwarebytes installed — this setting does not appear. Defender is already doing continuous scanning and Periodic Scanning is not needed.

How to check it yourself

  1. Press the Windows key, type Windows Security, and press Enter.
  2. Click Virus & threat protection.
  3. Scroll down to Microsoft Defender Antivirus options. If you see this section, another antivirus is active and Periodic Scanning is available.
  4. Click Microsoft Defender Antivirus options to expand it.
  5. Confirm Periodic scanning is set to On. If it says Off, turn it on.
Do not see Microsoft Defender Antivirus options? That means Defender is your primary antivirus and is already scanning continuously. Periodic Scanning does not apply to you — this is normal and correct.

What to expect

With Periodic Scanning on, Defender runs a background scan roughly once a month. You will not notice it during normal use — it runs quietly when your PC is idle. If it finds something, it will alert you just like any Defender notification. It does not conflict with Malwarebytes or slow down your PC in any meaningful way.

Recommended scan schedule alongside Periodic Scanning: Run a Malwarebytes Custom Scan (with rootkit scanning checked) once a month, and a Malwarebytes Deep Scan quarterly. Periodic Scanning adds Defender’s monthly check on top of that, giving you the best coverage from both programs.
