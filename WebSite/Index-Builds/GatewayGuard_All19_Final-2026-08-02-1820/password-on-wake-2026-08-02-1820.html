Password Required on Wake

When your PC wakes from sleep, it should ask for your sign-in before showing your desktop. Without this, anyone who walks up to your sleeping laptop can use it immediately — no password required.

✎ You set this up

What Checkup found and did

Found:Checkup reviews the sign-in requirement when your PC wakes from sleep. It can turn that on with your permission.
Action taken:Checkup reviews the setting and flags it if sign-in on wake is not required. This is a manual setup — the steps below show you exactly how to turn it on.

Why this matters

Sleep is not the same as off. When a laptop lid is closed or a PC goes to sleep, it looks off but all your open files, browser tabs, and accounts are still active in memory. If someone opens the lid and there is no sign-in prompt, they walk straight into your session — email, banking, everything.

This setting makes sure your PC asks for your PIN or password every time it wakes up. It takes two seconds and closes a gap that is easy to overlook.

How to set it up

  1. Press the Windows key, type Sign-in options, and press Enter.
  2. Under Require sign-in, click the dropdown.
  3. Select When PC wakes from sleep.
  4. Close Settings. The change takes effect immediately — no restart needed.

How to check if it is already on

  1. Press Windows key, type Sign-in options, press Enter.
  2. Under Require sign-in, check what the dropdown shows. It should say When PC wakes from sleep. If it says Never, follow the steps above to change it.

What to expect

The next time your PC wakes from sleep, it will show the lock screen and ask for your PIN, fingerprint, or password before continuing. This applies when you close the laptop lid, press the power button briefly, or the PC goes to sleep automatically after a period of inactivity.

Also check your sleep timer. If your PC never goes to sleep on its own, this setting has less effect. Open Settings > System > Power and make sure your PC is set to sleep after 10–15 minutes of inactivity. That way the lock kicks in automatically when you step away.
