## FLAGGED CONFLICTS

**Backup copy vs. single-PC license.** Q5 grants a second copy for
backup. Pricing is a $19.99 single-PC license. "Second copy" must be
defined as *an archival copy of the files* rather than *a second
installation*, or the two provisions contradict each other.

**No-refund policy vs. payment processor.** Gumroad enforces its own
refund terms, and state consumer-protection statutes may override
contract language regardless of what the agreement says. Whatever refund
conditions come out of action item 2 must be checked against both. This
overlaps the existing open question of Gumroad vs. direct checkout,
already routed to LegalZoom.
