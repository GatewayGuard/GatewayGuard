Yes, there are multiple ways to integrate a Gumroad product onto your webpage depending on how you want customers to interact with the checkout process.

### 1. Direct Text Link or Custom Button (Redirect)

This is the simplest method. You link any standard HTML button, text link, or image on your site directly to your Gumroad product URL (e.g., `[https://yourname.gumroad.com/l/productname](https://yourname.gumroad.com/l/productname)`).

* **How it works:** When clicked, the user leaves your website entirely and is redirected to your checkout page hosted securely on Gumroad.

### 2. Modal Overlay / Pop-up Widget

This keeps users on your site by triggering an interactive checkout overlay when they click a button.

* **How it works:** You grab the modal overlay snippet from your Gumroad account's widget generator and paste it into your webpage's HTML. It typically requires adding a small script tag to your header and giving your CTA button a specific class (like `class="gumroad-button"`). Clicking it pops up the secure checkout modal right over your page.

### 3. Inline Embed

If you want the product's entire preview card, description, and purchase form to live directly inside your webpage layout without a pop-up:

* **How it works:** You select the **Embed** option in the Gumroad widget builder, which generates a comprehensive HTML/JavaScript block. Paste that snippet directly into a custom HTML section of your site builder, and the full product widget will render inline.

*(Note: Many modern site builders—such as Carrd, WordPress plugins, or Webflow—also have native Gumroad blocks or elements where you simply paste your product link or ID, and the platform automatically configures the overlay or embed code for you.)*
