## HOW TO USE THIS SHEET

Add a row every time you identify a potential tester -- a conversation,
a family member, a neighbor, anyone who fits the target persona
(non-technical or semi-technical home Windows 11 user).

**Status values:**
- `Identified` -- noted as a candidate, not yet approached
- `Asked` -- you've mentioned beta testing to them
- `Confirmed` -- they've agreed to test when ready
- `Active` -- currently testing
- `Complete` -- finished a test round, feedback logged

## WHY FAMILY TESTERS MATTER

Beyond convenience, family testers double as an early read on the
**Family IT Hero** dynamic already identified in ProjectNotes -- if
your own children/grandchildren find the tool clear and non-intimidating,
that's a strong signal for the broader non-technical audience. Worth
noting in their feedback specifically: did they need YOU in the room,
or could they run it unassisted after reading the on-screen guidance?
