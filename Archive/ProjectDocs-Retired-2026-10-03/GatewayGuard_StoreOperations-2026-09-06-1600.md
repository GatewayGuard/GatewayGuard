# Store operations -- how a fix reaches a buyer, and how the annual stays separate

- **Document Name:** GatewayGuard_StoreOperations
- **Answers Bill's licence question 13:** *"How are we going to do this and
  control the versions?"*
- **Built on Bill's own Gumroad research**, 2026-09-06, plus verification of
  the one claim his research did not cover.
- **This is not a licence question.** The agreement text does not change. This
  is how the store is operated.

---

# 1. THE THREE THINGS GUMROAD GIVES US

*Sourced, Gumroad Help Center and Bill's research:*

| | What it does | Gated? |
|---|---|---|
| **The file on the product** | Replace it and every future download serves the new one | No |
| **The update email** | Emails past buyers a download link when you save a change | **Yes -- see section 3** |
| **The buyer's own link and library** | *Sourced:* customers **re-download the updated file from their purchase link or account library** | No |

***Sourced, Gumroad:*** updating a product **does not change its URL** and does
not affect any link to it. So nothing on our website ever needs re-pointing
when we ship a fix.

> **The important one is the third row.** Even with no email, **a buyer who
> goes back to their receipt link gets the current file.** That is the
> mechanism that always works.

---

# 2. HOW A CORRECTION REACHES SOMEONE WHO ALREADY BOUGHT

**A correction is a fix to the version they bought -- not a new annual
version.** The licence already says they are entitled to it at no charge.

1. **Replace the file on the Checkup product.** Do not add it alongside.
2. **Send the update email** if the account is past the $100 gate.
3. **The link keeps working regardless**, which is the backstop.

## ONE RULE THAT MATTERS MORE THAN IT LOOKS: REPLACE, NEVER ACCUMULATE

***Sourced, Gumroad's own instructions:*** the update flow is worded as **"Add
more files."** Left alone, that means a product page can end up offering
**several versions at once**, and the buyer picks one.

> **For a security tool that is a defect, not a feature.** A customer who
> downloads last season's file gets the settings we already corrected, and
> neither they nor we would know. **Exactly one Checkup file is on the product
> at any time.**

**And the file carries its version in its name**, so a customer who has two
copies in their Downloads folder can tell which is which without opening
either.

---

# 3. THE $100 GATE -- KNOW IT NOW, NOT ON LAUNCH DAY

***Sourced, Bill's research, confirmed as a Gumroad anti-spam rule:*** a new
seller account **cannot send broadcast or update emails to customers until it
has earned $100 after fees and taken its first payout.** It is a **one-time**
gate. Once passed, it stays passed, and there is no limit afterwards.

## WHAT THIS MEANS IN PRACTICE

**Our earliest customers are the ones we cannot email.** They are also the ones
most likely to hit a defect worth correcting, because they are running the
build that has had the least field exposure.

> **The answer is not to fix Gumroad. It is to tell the customer up front how
> updates reach them**, so they know to check rather than waiting for a mail
> that will not come.

**The sentence, for the store page and the receipt:**

> *"Your download link always gives you the current version of Checkup. If we
> issue a correction, use the same link from your receipt and you will get the
> corrected file at no charge."*

**That is true on day one and true forever**, with or without the email. **It
does not promise an email we cannot yet send** -- which is the same standard
we applied to the per-PC enforcement sentence in question 10.

---

# 4. KEEPING THE ANNUAL VERSION A SEPARATE PURCHASE

**The licence says the annual updated version is a separate product and a
separate purchase.** For that to be true in fact and not just on paper:

| Do this | Because |
|---|---|
| **The annual version is its own Gumroad product**, with its own page and price | A new product on the same product page is an update, and Gumroad would give it to past buyers free -- which would contradict the licence |
| **Never put the annual file on the existing Checkup product** | One save would hand a paid annual version to every past buyer at no charge, and it could not be taken back |
| **The existing product's page says plainly what it is** | *"This is the 2026 version. It keeps working. A version for a new Windows release is sold separately."* |

> **The whole risk here is one wrong upload.** A correction and an annual
> version are handled by two different actions on two different products, and
> the difference is worth writing on a sticky note before the first one is
> ever done.

**And the customer is not left guessing:** *sourced, Gumroad:* the licence's
promise that *"we will never make your existing copy stop working"* is simply
true -- **their file is a file they already downloaded.** Nothing we do at the
store can reach it.

---

# 5. VERSION CONTROL, IN FOUR RULES

1. **One file on the product at a time.**
2. **The version is in the filename**, so a customer can tell two downloads
   apart.
3. **A correction replaces. An annual version is a new product.** Never the
   other way round.
4. **Keep a record of what was on the store and when** -- date, version,
   what changed, and whether the update email went out. **Without it, a
   support email saying "it does not do X" cannot be answered**, because we
   will not know which file they have.

> **Rule 4 is the one that will be skipped and the one that will be missed.**
> It is a text file with four columns, kept in `ProjectDocs\`. **I will start
> it the day the store opens.**

# 6. WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE

**The refund window interacts with all of this and I have not worked it
through here.** A customer inside their 14 days who receives a corrected file
-- does the window run from the original purchase or from the correction?
**The licence says 14 days from buying, which is clear, but a customer who
hits a defect on day 13 and gets a fix on day 15 will not read it that way.**

**That one is genuinely a question for the attorney**, and it is going into
the consult list rather than being decided here.
