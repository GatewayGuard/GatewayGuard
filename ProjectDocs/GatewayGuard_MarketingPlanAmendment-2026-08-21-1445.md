## 2. THE CORRECTION THE PRICING DECISION FORCES

**The plan's own recommended footer line is now wrong.**

Section 2, *"One line to settle across every surface"*, currently recommends:

> One-time purchase. No subscription. Annual updates are optional.

**`GatewayGuard_CloudRequest-PricingCopy-2026-08-21.md` section 1 instructs the
opposite:** remove every *"no subscription / no renewal / no ongoing fees / no
upsell"* claim, because renewals are now sold as plans on the website.

**Both documents are live and they disagree.** The plan reasoned that optional
updates are not a subscription, which was true on 2026-08-19. **Pre-paid
multi-year renewal plans are close enough to one that the distinction is
lawyer's ground, not marketing ground** — and the plan's own standard is that a
claim which invites an easy disproof lands on the single thing being sold.

**Recommended replacement line:**

> One-time purchase, yours to keep. Annual updates are optional.
