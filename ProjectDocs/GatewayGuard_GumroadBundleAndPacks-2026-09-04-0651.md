## READ THIS BEFORE YOU PASTE -- FOUR THINGS

**1. The packs are NOT a bundle. They are versions of Checkup.**
A Gumroad bundle combines *different* products. The 3 / 5 / 10-PC packs are the
same product with more licences, so they belong in the **Versions** list on the
Checkup product -- which is what **T-PACK** already says. Part 2 below is written
as versions.

**2. The bundle's refund is still an open decision, and the screen shows a third
answer that is in neither draft.** Your screenshot shows the bundle set to
**"30-day money back guarantee."** ***measured, licence v3.0:*** Checkup is **14
days**, the Guide is **no refund**, and 30 days appears nowhere any more. The
bundle DECISION NEEDED offers 14 days or none. **Both refund blocks are written
out in Part 1. Pick one; do not leave 30 days.**

**3. ***Not measured: how a bundle buyer picks their print size.*** Under Option
B a Guide buyer chooses one of five, at purchase. A bundle that simply contains
"the Guide" has nowhere to make that choice, and would either deliver the wrong
size or all five. **The fix that keeps the promise: give the bundle the same five
versions**, each holding Checkup's file plus that one Guide PDF. Part 1 covers
it. Check this before publishing.
