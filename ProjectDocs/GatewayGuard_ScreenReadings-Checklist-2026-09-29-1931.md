## Found along the way -- for the guide (Cloud)

1. **Part 1 dead end:** when protection is Off, **Create** is grey. The guide needs a step: *Configure > Turn on system protection > OK*, then Create. (C1, C1b)
2. **Setting 1 undo:** Windows Update's pause is now "**Select the date to pause updates until**", a calendar up to about 5 weeks ahead. There is no "Pause for 1 week" button. (C4)
3. **Setting 17 undo:** the choices are Never, Every Time, 1, 3, 5 and 15 minutes. "Choose **Never**" is not the right undo; the reader should pick the time they had before. (C6; triage G4)
4. **Setting 8 on Pro:** the page shows "Windows (C:) BitLocker suspended" with **Resume protection**. The guide should tell a reader who sees "suspended" what to do. (C5)
5. **Setting 13 undo (new):** after Checkup, both Edge switches carry a **briefcase icon** -- Edge's sign that a setting is controlled by policy -- so the reader cannot simply turn them back on in Edge. The guide's undo must say so and give the way back. (C12)
6. **Protection history (new):** an empty list reads "**No recent actions**". The guide should say that this means nothing was found, so the reader is not left wondering. (C8, S7)
7. **Quick Assist (new):** the first screen can open with "**Let Quick Assist access your camera?**" The guide should say what to answer (**No** is safe; it is not needed to get help). (C11)
8. **Lock screen (new):** Windows key + L shows "**Enter your PIN**" and "**Sign-in options**" (key = password, keypad = PIN), and **no "I forgot my PIN" link** on CGDELL. There is nothing to cancel -- the reader signs back in. Use these exact words; do not mention a "forgot PIN" link. (S2, Bill's photo)
9. **Setting 6 labels (new):** the three "warn me" boxes are "Warn me about malicious apps and sites", "Warn me about password reuse", "Warn me about unsafe password storage". On CGDELL the last two are **not ticked**. Use these exact labels in the guide. (C14)
10. **Widgets check (new):** with Widgets off, **Windows key + W does nothing** -- a quick way for a reader to confirm it. (C13, Bill)
11. **Setting 10 on Home (new):** Settings > System > Remote Desktop **exists** on Home but has **no switch** -- it says "Your Home edition of Windows 11 doesn't support Remote Desktop." The guide should tell a Home reader that this message means they are already protected and there is nothing to do. It matches Checkup's "N/A on Home -- GOOD". (S3)
