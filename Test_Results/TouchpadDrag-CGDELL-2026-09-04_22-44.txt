-- WHY THE MOUSE OBEYED AND THE TOUCHPAD DID NOT ----------------------

  They are two different mechanisms, and only one of them reads the
  drag threshold.

    The MOUSE asks Windows how far you moved before it decides you
    meant to drag. That is the drag threshold, and raising it works.

    The TOUCHPAD has its own gesture -- tap twice and hold -- and the
    touchpad driver starts the drag itself. It never asks Windows
    about the threshold, so no number could ever have fixed it.

-- BEFORE -------------------------------------------------------------
  TapAndDrag   : 1   (1 = on, 0 = off)
  AAPThreshold : 2   (touchpad sensitivity -- reported only)

-- TURNING TAP-AND-DRAG OFF ------------------------------------------

  What you keep: tapping the touchpad still clicks. Pressing the
  touchpad down and moving still drags, deliberately.

  What stops: a light double-tap will no longer pick a folder up
  and carry it.

-- AFTER --------------------------------------------------------------
  TapAndDrag   : 0

  WRITTEN AND READ BACK.

-- IT MAY NEED A SIGN-OUT, AND HERE IS HOW TO TELL --------------------

  The touchpad driver reads this when it starts. If tap-and-drag still
  works right now, sign out and back in, then try again.

  To see it in Windows: Settings > Bluetooth and devices > Touchpad,
  then open Taps. The box for tapping twice and dragging should now be
  clear. If Settings still shows it ticked, sign out and back in.

-- THE OTHER LEVER, REPORTED AND NOT TOUCHED --------------------------

  Touchpad sensitivity is currently 2.

  A higher number makes the touchpad ignore lighter contact, which
  helps if a palm or a sleeve is brushing it. It also makes deliberate
  taps harder to land, so it is a genuine trade-off rather than a
  free improvement -- which is why this script leaves it alone.

  To change it yourself: Settings > Bluetooth and devices > Touchpad,
  and pick from the Touchpad sensitivity list. Try one step at a time.

-- TO UNDO ------------------------------------------------------------
  Double-click Run-SetTouchpadDrag-Revert.bat.

-- IF A FOLDER EVER DOES MOVE BY ACCIDENT -----------------------------
  Press Ctrl+Z in File Explorer straight away. That undoes the move
  and puts the folder back where it was.
