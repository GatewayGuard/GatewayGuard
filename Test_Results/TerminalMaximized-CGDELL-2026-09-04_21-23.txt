  3. REPORTED, NOT CHANGED -- QuickEdit mode
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  QuickEdit, the default for all console windows : 1
  QuickEdit, PowerShell only : 1

  QuickEdit ON means clicking inside a console window starts selecting
  text, and while text is selected the program STOPS until you press
  Escape or Enter. That is FT-63, and it is the most likely reason a
  window looks frozen after you click in it.

  Checkup protects itself -- it re-asserts the console flags before
  every read. Your own PowerShell window does not.

  Not changed here on purpose: turning it off costs you click-to-select,
  which is a real loss. To turn it off by hand: click the icon at the
  top left of the window, choose Properties, and clear QuickEdit Mode.
