  2. THE THREE THINGS THAT CAN DEFEAT A CORRECT DRAG THRESHOLD
======================================================================

  A 30-pixel threshold is not the whole story. Each of these can still
  let a folder move, and none of them is obvious.

  -- ClickLock (Settings, Mouse, Turn on ClickLock) -------------------
     stored : (not set)   hold time : 1200 ms
     Off. Good -- this is not what is moving your folders.

  -- Touchpad tap-and-drag -------------------------------------------
     TapAndDrag : 1   (1 = on, 0 = off)
     ON. A tap-and-hold on the touchpad starts a drag by a
     different route. If folders move when you are using the
     touchpad and not the mouse, this is the likely cause.

  -- Double-click speed, as a drag cause ------------------------------
     stored : 650 ms   in force : 650 ms
     Comfortable. Not a cause.
