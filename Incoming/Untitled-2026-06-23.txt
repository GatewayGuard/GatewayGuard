Here's what the research confirms:

Malwarebytes Free has zero real-time protection — it's a manual scanner only. When the 14-day Premium Trial expires it automatically reverts to free with no charge and no real-time. You can also deactivate the trial manually anytime.

Tamper Protection — Malwarebytes Free does NOT touch Defender's Tamper Protection. The reason item 3 was showing "OFF" is because the Premium Trial was active, which puts Defender into passive mode. When Defender goes passive, its Tamper Protection value becomes unreadable/unreliable. In free/companion mode, Tamper Protection can be checked and enabled normally.

Firewall — Windows Firewall is always managed by Windows (MpsSvc service), never by Malwarebytes regardless of free or trial state. Keep Malwarebytes Free — yes, because:

It catches PUPs, adware, and browser hijackers that Defender regularly misses
In free mode it doesn't touch Defender, Tamper Protection, or the firewall
Defender + Malwarebytes Free is the recommended setup for personal Windows PCs

Auto monthly scan with email/text alerts — we can't do that with the free version, because:

Malwarebytes Free is manual only — no scheduled scans, no automated runs, no alerting
Scheduled scanning and email/text alerts are Premium-only features
The free version requires the user to open it and click Scan themselves

Use Windows Task Scheduler to open Malwarebytes at a set time each month so it's ready — but the user still has to click Scan
