While LegalZoom does not offer a single, comprehensive PDF "user manual" for your account, you can maximize your **Business Attorney Plan** (formerly Business Advisory Plan) by leveraging its core features strategically.

Here is a guide to making the most of your account benefits:

### 1. Attorney Consultations

* **Unlimited 30-Minute Consults:** You are entitled to unlimited 30-minute phone consultations on **new legal matters**. Because each topic must be distinct, divide complex multi-faceted issues (e.g., separating employment questions from contract drafting) into separate scheduled sessions.
* **Annual Legal Check-Up:** After maintaining your plan for 6 months, you unlock a 1-hour annual review with an attorney. Use this proactively to audit your compliance, review corporate minutes, or check risk exposure.
* **Discounted Representation:** If you need an attorney for ongoing litigation, court filings, or deep representation beyond the scope of plan consultations, you receive a **25% discount** on your network attorney's standard hourly rates.

### 2. Document Reviews

* **Contract & Form Reviews:** You can have an attorney review key business documents and contracts (up to 10 pages per document) to flag risks or compliance issues before you sign.
* **Customizable Legal Templates:** Your plan includes access to a library of over 150 legal templates (such as NDAs, independent contractor agreements, and leases) complete with a rich editor to tailor them to your operations.

### 3. Integrated Digital Tools

* **Unlimited eSignatures:** Utilize the built-in eSignature tool to securely execute documents with clients, partners, or vendors directly through your dashboard.
* **Copyright Registration:** Your subscription includes **one copyright registration per month**, which is useful if you are protecting software code, written guides, creative works, or documentation.
* **Product Discounts:** Enjoy a **10% discount** on other standalone LegalZoom services, such as trademark filings, state compliance renewals, or additional corporate amendments.

### Tips for Scheduling and Preparation

* **Be Specific in Your Intake:** When booking a call through your dashboard (like the consultation setup you have open), use the "Add more details" section to clearly state your questions ahead of time. Giving the attorney context before the call ensures you maximize the 30-minute limit.
* **Keep Records:** Remember that communications through the LegalZoom platform are designed for general guidance and business strategy. Keep copies of all reviewed contracts and consultation notes saved directly in your secure online dashboard for quick reference.
